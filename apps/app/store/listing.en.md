## Description (4000 char max)

```
Lucid Me is a dream journal and a practice tool for lucid dreaming, grounded in techniques studied by research.

YOUR DREAM GARDEN
Every dream you record becomes a unique visual organism in your garden: color comes from emotion, luminescence from lucidity, complexity from the words you wrote. The garden grows night after night, telling the story of your practice. Tap an organism to re-read the dream.

DAWN CAPTURE
Right after waking, record the dream by voice or in writing. Add the emotion and lucidity level with a tap. The app recognizes your recurring dream signs.

A GUIDED PATH
An adaptive 21-day path takes you from foundations (recall, reality checks) to advanced techniques:
• MILD — pre-sleep intention (LaBerge)
• WBTB — Wake Back To Bed
• SSILD — Senses Initiated Lucid Dream
• TLR — Targeted Lucidity Reactivation (Konkoly 2024)
Every lesson cites its source. No promises: only practice.

THE NIGHT RITUAL
Three gestures before sleep: MILD intention, TLR audio training (20 min), review of dream signs. A reliable WBTB alarm with customizable sounds.

THE LIGHTS OF PROGRESS
Watch your journey: lucid dream frequency, recall score, streak, most recurrent dream signs. Honest numbers, computed on your device.

REALITY CHECK
Randomized notifications and 50+ varied prompts to train the habit of asking "am I dreaming?" — anti-habituation by design.

PRIVACY AS A CORE VALUE
Lucid Me is local-first: your dreams live only on your device, in a local database. No cloud, no mandatory account. Analytics are optional and anonymous; dream content is never collected or analyzed. You can export your data to JSON or Markdown anytime, even on the free plan.

ON THE SCIENCE
The techniques included are grounded in lucid-dream research. We cite the studies (LaBerge, Konkoly 2024) and make no claims about binaural beats or unsupported methods. Lucid dreams are not guaranteed: they depend on consistent practice.

Lucid Me is a practice tool and a personal journal. It is not medical therapy and does not replace professional advice.
```
